How Scores Work

Combined Score range: 0 to 3 per hex.

+1 if at least one grocery/convenience store is within 800m.

+1 if at least one park is within 800m.

+1 if at least one library is within 1200m.

Why Hexes

Hexes make coverage easier to compare across communities and reduce directional bias versus square grids.

For this map, hexes summarize nearby access consistently: 800m for grocery/convenience and parks, 1200m for libraries.

Reference: ArcGIS: Why hexagons?
