How Scores Work

Combined Score range: 0 to 3 per hex.

+1 if at least one grocery/convenience store is within 800m.

+1 if at least one park is within 800m.

+1 if at least one library is within 1200m.

Why Hexes Help

Hexes make coverage easier to compare across communities and reduce directional bias versus square grids.

Reference: ArcGIS: Why hexagons?.

For this walkshed map, hexes are used to summarize nearby access (800m for grocery/convenience and parks, 1200m for libraries) in a consistent way.

The hex size was chosen to balance local detail with stability: small enough to show community-level change, but large enough to avoid block-by-block noise.
